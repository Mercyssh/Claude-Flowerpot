Pick a Flower
for Yourself

In the quiet where the morning pools,

a small green promise keeps its counsel.

You reached, and it remembered how to open —

petal by patient petal, unafraid.

Carry it in the soft dark of your hair,

a lantern for the hours that forget the sun.

And when the road grows long and colorless,

let this one bloom insist that you continue.

Happy Girlfriend Day!
